# Validation

Every computation is checked against an independent reference: textbook worked examples (to their printed
precision), NASA GMAT, Skyfield, astropy, and real missions. The test suite runs these comparisons on every
commit (Python 3.10–3.13, newest and oldest supported dependencies).

## NASA GMAT R2026a

Maximum position difference over the run, in the frame whose z axis is Earth's true pole (GMAT evaluates gravity
about the true spin axis; urania about the frame's z axis, 7.69" apart at J2000):

| scenario | duration | difference | size of the effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| two-body | 1 day | 0.009 m | – |
| J2 | 7 days | 3.3 m | – |
| J2 + exponential drag | 3 days | 3.3 m | – |
| J2 + NRLMSISE-00 drag (F10.7 150, Kp 3) | 3 days | 15 m | 253 km |
| zonal J2–J6, 600 km | 7 days | 2.5 m | – |
| Sun + Moon, 20 200 km | 7 days | 18.7 m (GMAT: DE405; urania: analytic Moon) | 7.9 km |
| radiation pressure, GEO | 7 days | 0.49 m | 3.2 km |

The GMAT scripts are generated by `validation/gmat/make_scripts.py`; rerun them with
`GmatConsole.exe --run <script>` and `pytest tests/test_gmat.py` compares the output.
